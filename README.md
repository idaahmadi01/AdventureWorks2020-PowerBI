# Adventure Works | Power BI Dashboard

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built using the Adventure Works dataset. The goal was to turn sales data into a clear, easy-to-navigate report that makes it easier to explore business performance, customer behavior, and product profitability.

The dashboard analyzes **$29.36M in sales, 28K orders, $9M in profit, and 18.48K customers**, with an overall **profit margin of 30.65%**.

## Dashboard Overview

The report is organized into three pages:

- **Sales Overview:** Tracks sales, orders, profit, and revenue trends, with breakdowns by product category and country.
- **Customer Analysis:** Explores purchasing behavior and demographics across **18.48K customers**, including age groups, income levels, occupations, and monthly customer growth.
- **Product Analysis:** Examines **397 products**, highlighting best-selling items, sales by subcategory, profit margins, and detailed product performance.

## Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI Desktop & Power Query:** Data preparation and interactive reporting.
- **DAX:** Custom measures for sales, profit, profit margin, customer growth, and purchasing patterns.
- **Data Modeling:** Relationships between sales, customer, product, calendar, and territory tables.
- **Data Visualization:** KPI cards, trend charts, category comparisons, and product-level tables.

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for date, region, category, and subcategory, along with dynamic page navigation, reset filters, and custom tooltips.

My focus was on building a report that not only presents the numbers but also makes it easy to explore trends, compare performance, and understand different areas of the business.
